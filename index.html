          Algorithm notes ↗
          

            Conventions & limits
            

              Rows are basis vectors. Coordinates satisfy v = zB, with integer
              z. Membership and search distances use integer arithmetic. LLL and
              Babai use floating-point Gram–Schmidt calculations.
            

            

              Enumeration uses a certified coefficient box, with a time limit
              and a ceiling of two million tuples. It claims optimality only
              after checking the whole box. Displayed lattice points are a
              finite sample; higher-dimensional views are projections.
            

            

              Input must be a full-rank square integer matrix. This is a
              teaching and debugging tool for small examples, not a
              cryptanalytic solver.
